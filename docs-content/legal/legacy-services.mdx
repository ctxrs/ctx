# Legacy services and support

Blame is included in the open-source ctx CLI from 1.5. It needs no account or
subscription. See [Blame](/blame) for current use.

For an existing account, subscription, invoice, referral balance, or payout,
contact [support@ctx.rs](mailto:support@ctx.rs). For hosted-data requests,
contact [privacy@ctx.rs](mailto:privacy@ctx.rs). Installing or upgrading ctx does
not itself cancel an existing subscription or delete hosted records. Local
legacy data and keys are left untouched. This page does not start a trial,
create a referral, or change earlier billing obligations.

## Earlier referral terms

The following records the referral terms published August 27, 2026 for the
earlier offering. It preserves the conditions for existing activity; it is not
an invitation to purchase or refer. Earlier codenames belonged to verified
identities and were immutable. Attribution was direct and first-accepted,
without referral links, cookies, or installer parameters. The program covered
ordinary verified-person referrals, not creator or paid-media affiliate activity.

The earlier offering had a 14-day anonymous trial or an eligible 30-day
referred trial. Its subscription price was $20 USD monthly;
there is no annual plan. For one direct referral, each distinct qualifying paid
invoice for that monthly subscription can accrue a $10 USD cash commission for
invoices 1 through 12, with a maximum commission of $120 USD. In integer
currency amounts, the qualifying base price is 2,000 cents, the commission per
invoice is 1,000 cents, and the per-referral cap is 12,000 cents. Separately
assessed Stripe tax does not change the 2,000-cent base-price test. Credited,
discounted, partially paid, zero-value, prorated, duplicate, wrong-price,
refunded, or disputed invoices do not qualify.

Invoice 1 and invoice 2 each accrue a separate pending $10 commission. Neither
is eligible for payment until invoice 2 settles, a 14-day hold has passed from
that settlement, and ctx completes authoritative reconciliation of both
invoices. Each commission for invoices 3 through 12 has its own 14-day hold
from that invoice's settlement and its own authoritative reconciliation.
Hold expiry never approves or pays a commission automatically.

Self-referrals, fake or duplicate accounts, spam, cookie stuffing,
misrepresentation, and other attempts to manipulate attribution or commissions
are prohibited. Referrals between people at the same company are allowed,
subject to each person's employer policies and other obligations. When sharing
a codename publicly, disclose clearly that you may receive a commission. You
are responsible for taxes and reporting obligations associated with
commissions.

Refunds, payment disputes, fraud, policy violations, or invalid attribution
void affected unpaid commissions. If an affected commission has already been
paid, its reversal becomes a negative adjustment or debt, is deducted from
future aggregate referral earnings, and requires manual review. ctx will not
seek an external clawback of that already-paid commission.

Payout onboarding is completed through Stripe's hosted flow. At launch, each
referrer may create one individual Stripe payout-recipient relationship only;
there is no company payout path. Interactive setup collects the recipient's
country before creation, while advanced machine-readable setup may supply a
two-letter country code. ctx does not infer country from identity,
organization, email, IP address, billing information, or any other signal.
Stripe collects the recipient's identity, tax, bank, and other sensitive payout
details. Invoice-level commissions and commissions across referrals are
combined into aggregate manual payout batches, not separate onboarding
relationships. Every payout requires authoritative reconciliation and manual
action. Its timing depends on that review, Stripe onboarding and availability,
the referrer's aggregate adjustment balance, and payment-network processing.
Referral status is private and aggregate and does not identify referred people
or expose their invoices.

Aggregate status distinguishes `pending_cents`, `manual_review_cents`,
`payable_cents`, `processing_cents`, `paid_cents`, and `debt_cents`.
`manual_review_cents` is accrued commission awaiting an explicit review
outcome. `processing_cents` is a sent payout batch that has not settled.
`paid_cents` is historical cash actually settled and does not decrease after a
reversal. A post-paid reversal instead increases the nonnegative `debt_cents`
balance. Every status satisfies:

```text
earned_cents + debt_cents
  = pending_cents + manual_review_cents + payable_cents
    + processing_cents + paid_cents
```

Future earnings reduce debt before they become payable. There is no external
clawback.

We may change or end the referral program prospectively. Updated terms apply
from their stated effective date to qualifying invoices that settle on or
after that date, subject to applicable law. Refunds, disputes, negative
adjustments, fraud, and the other conditions above may still affect an earlier
commission.

## Earlier service data

The following preserves the August 28, 2026 disclosure for earlier service
activity. ctx 1.5 does not create trial claims, collect machine fingerprints,
read legacy keys, or require these services. Existing hosted records remain
subject to the applicable privacy policy and deletion requests.

In releases before 1.5, trial activation was a network exception to local
setup. The CLI contacted the first-party anonymous-trial service to establish
and evaluate the trial and stores installation-bound trial and anti-rollback
state in the operating system's native key store. That state is not transcript
content, a graph key, an account token, or an entitlement body. Declining the
trial does not make this trial-service request.

The release-signed trial helper derives challenge- and installation-bound
opaque digests from a random anchor stored in the native key store and
available OS-install, firmware, and board identifiers. Raw identifiers are
normalized and application-HMACed locally; they are not sent to ctx or logged.
The anonymous-trial service applies an independent, versioned server HMAC and
retains only anti-repeat lookup tokens and the minimal deadline and lifecycle
data needed to enforce the one-trial policy.

This is best-effort abuse prevention, not hardware identity or attestation.
Raw anonymous-device evidence and its anti-repeat fingerprint are not joined to
analytics, history, paths, identity, or billing data. A confirmed
legacy scoped data-deletion command removes the client anchor and trial
credentials from local native-vault storage, but it does not remove the minimal
server-side consumed-trial tombstone retained for the one-trial policy.

## Referrals and payouts

Referral codenames are public. Do not put private information in a codename.
For earlier explicitly referred trials, we keep a bounded referral ledger to
apply one direct immutable attribution, verify qualifying paid invoices, and
administer invoice-level commissions. The ledger may contain public codename
ownership; the relevant WorkOS subject and personal-organization coordinates;
a verified-email digest; trial claim and attribution identifiers; Stripe
customer, subscription, invoice, charge, payment-intent, recipient, payout, and
payout-batch references; bounded idempotency and webhook-event records; and the
timestamps, amounts, invoice ordinals, hold deadlines, reconciliation results,
review reasons, commission revisions, negative adjustments or debt, and payout
state needed to administer the program.

The referral ledger does not contain transcripts, prompts, source code, Git
data, repository data, raw anonymous-device evidence or fingerprints, payment
card details, or bank details. At launch, payout recipients are individual
people only. Before creating the single Stripe recipient, interactive setup
collects the person's country; an advanced machine-readable caller may supply
a two-letter country code. ctx does not infer country from identity,
organization, email, IP address, billing information, or any other signal.
Stripe collects payout onboarding, identity, tax, and bank details in its
hosted flow; ctx receives only the recipient reference and payout status needed
to administer aggregate manual payout batches. ctx does not create a new
payout-recipient relationship for every invoice or referral.

An accepted referral claim is separate from anonymous-device evidence. If the
referred trial converts to a paid subscription, that explicit claim can be
joined to the WorkOS and Stripe references needed for attribution and
commission processing. The underlying raw device evidence and fingerprint
remain unjoined from identity and billing.

Referral status is available only to the authenticated referrer and is
aggregate. It does not identify referred people or expose their invoice-level
activity. Its accounting separates accrued cash awaiting manual review,
payable cash, sent-but-unsettled processing cash, historical settled cash, and
the nonnegative debt balance created by post-paid reversals. ctx does not use a
referral site, tracking link, browser cookie, or creator-affiliate tracking
profile for attribution.
